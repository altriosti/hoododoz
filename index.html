CPU or GPU?

GPU, if your browser offers it (Chrome and Edge do). A graphics card tries hundreds of times more numbers per second. CPU still works and the target eases off by itself when the field goes quiet. Keep the tab open while you mine.

Why did my solution stop working?

Someone else minted first. Every hood is chained to the one before it, so when anyone mints, every search starts again on the new one. The site checks every find before your wallet opens, so nothing is spent.

Why does the difficulty change?

It aims for one hood every 10 seconds, retargets every 8 mints, halves after every 5 idle minutes, and gets harder for a miner who wins several in a row.

What does the team get?

10% of every mint, the 5% trading fee on $HOODZ and 50,000 $HOODZ at launch. The pool's liquidity has no way out. The contracts include an owner-only emergency withdraw and pause for mint, for safety only.
